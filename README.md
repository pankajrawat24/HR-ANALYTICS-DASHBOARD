🚀 Unveiling My New HR Analytics Dashboard! 🚀

Ever wondered what drives employee attrition in an organization? I’ve developed a comprehensive HR Analytics Dashboard that reveals key insights into workforce trends, helping HR professionals make data-driven decisions.

Key Metric Analyzed : ❓

1.Attrition Rate 📉

2.Average Salary 💰

3.Average Years at company 🫂

4.Attrition by departments 🏢

Key Insights: 💡

🔸It being found that employees in Life science field seems to be leaving the company at large numbers.

🔸Around 116 Employees have left company between the age group of 26-35 this age group Records the highest Attrition Rate.

🔸Another Main Reason for Higher Attrition Rate is that the employees are being paid less than the company's Avg salary.

🔸Attrition Rate is seems to be higher in sales Department than other 2 departments.

🔸Laboratory Technicians and sales executive are the 2 job roles where the employees felt most dissatisfied with their roles this may led the employee to leave.

🔸Employees with 5 years of service at the company showed larger attrition rate.

🔸Overall male employees tend to leave the company more often then the female employees.
